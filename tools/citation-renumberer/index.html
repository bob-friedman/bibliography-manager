Pro Citation Renumbering Tool

Interconvert numbered citations and short-form tags for easy editing.

Workflow:
1. Paste the manuscript text and numbered references into the boxes below.
2. Click 1. Convert Numbers to Tags. This replaces [1, 2] with [Smith 2020; Doe 2019].
3. Rearrange, add, or delete text in the manuscript as needed.
4. Click 2. Convert Tags to Numbers. This reassigns numbers chronologically and re-sorts the reference list. (Unused references are appended at the bottom in original order).

Notes: Ranges accept hyphens, en-dashes (–), em-dashes (—) and minus signs. Years 1000–2999 are recognized (e.g. 1847, 1894 work). Duplicate author-year tags get suffixes a, b, c…
